The Founder

Dejan
Simović

Founder · Breeder · Trainer · International Judge

Dejan Simović is an internationally recognized dog breeder and trainer with more than 30 years of experience working with German Shepherds and other working breeds. He is the founder of the Von Simpor kennel — bloodlines known worldwide for stable temperament, strong working drives, excellent anatomy, and reliability.

Throughout his career, Dejan has placed more than 20 dogs among the Top 10 at World Championships for German Shepherds. With his dogs he has achieved victories across nearly every continent — from Portugal and Spain to Sweden, South Korea, China, Mongolia, and throughout the Americas.

A special milestone: Paris Von Simpor became the first and only dog from Serbia in history to win at Crufts — the world's most prestigious all-breed dog show, held in the United Kingdom. In the Czech Republic, one of the strongest working dog nations, Dejan claimed the National Working Championship with Kendy Z. Kraje Husitu.

He holds certifications from several international canine organizations as both a helper in working dog sport and a K9 handler — enabling active cooperation in police K9 training and preparation of dogs for KKL, CTP, and other internationally recognized selections.

Our Mission

In Dejan's Own Words

Love for dogs began in my childhood, through my family. My grandfather worked with hunting dogs, while my father was devoted to breeding top-quality Setters, Pointers, and German Wirehaired Pointers. As a child, accompanying my father's dogs to competitions and shows, I developed a deep connection with dogs that gradually became my life's calling.

The German Shepherd was my greatest passion from the very beginning. I was fascinated by its beauty, anatomy, and exceptional working abilities. As I grew older, I decided to begin my own German Shepherd breeding program and pursue an independent career as a breeder.

Aware of the fact that Germany is the homeland of this breed, I realized that true progress and major success were possible only through working at the very center of the canine world. I spent much of my youth in Germany, training, learning, and competing alongside the world's best trainers at the most prestigious German Shepherd shows in the world.

Persistence, discipline, talent, and a strong desire led to results of which I am extremely proud today — both I and the dogs from my breeding program achieved top placements around the world. The German Shepherd, as one of the most recognized breeds globally, gave me the opportunity to meet friends and colleagues from all over the world and to compete on every continent.

I shared my knowledge and experience through numerous seminars held around the world, including in Germany, South Korea, and Mongolia. One of my greatest satisfactions is knowing that many professional trainers today work successfully and independently as a result of the knowledge and experience they gained through my school.

I continued this mission through Simpor Dog Center — with a clear desire to professionally help and train as many dogs and owners as possible, so that the bond between human and dog can achieve its true meaning.

After many years spent abroad, following the highest standards in breeding, training, and working dog programs, I made the decision to bring part of that knowledge, experience, and system of work back to Serbia. In my home region, where I spent my childhood and some of the most beautiful days of my life, in the heart of Šumadija and surrounded by untouched nature, a specially designed space was created for the accommodation, breeding, and professional training of dogs.

The sporting spirit still keeps me at the same level of dedication as at the beginning of my career. Maximum attention is focused on genetics, a high level of training, proper nutrition, and veterinary care, so that every dog can achieve its full potential. Together with my team, partners, and friends around the world, the family of Simpor Dog Center continues to grow every day. I will continue to do my best to justify the trust of future clients and their dogs.

Our Facility

Designed for Excellence

Simpor Dog Center is a specially designed complex dedicated to the breeding, accommodation, and professional training of dogs. Spread across more than two hectares of organized space, in the natural surroundings of Šumadija, the center provides conditions for safe, functional, and efficient work with dogs according to modern international standards.

The center features both outdoor and indoor training areas, designed to support work in all weather conditions and for different types of training — from obedience and sport disciplines to protection and K9 training.

In my home region, in the heart of Šumadija surrounded by untouched nature, a space was created that combines experience gained around the world, a natural environment, and functional infrastructure — with one goal: to provide every dog with the best possible conditions for development, work, and progress.
