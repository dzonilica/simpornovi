Expert Selection

How Selection Works

The Right Dog
for Your Purpose

Every dog we offer has been individually evaluated by our team. We do not list dogs like products — we match dogs to people. Before recommending a dog, we take the time to understand your goals, your experience, your lifestyle, and what you need from your dog.

Whether you're a professional handler preparing for World Championship competition, a security company seeking reliable K9 candidates, or a family looking for a confident, stable companion — the selection process at Simpor is always personal and professional.

All available dogs come from our breeding program or from carefully trusted partners, with full documentation, health clearances, and ongoing support after purchase.
