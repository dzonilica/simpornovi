Our Facility

Designed for Excellence

Simpor Dog Center is a specially designed complex dedicated to the breeding, accommodation, and professional training of dogs. Spread across more than two hectares of organized space, in the natural surroundings of Šumadija, the center provides conditions for safe, functional, and efficient work with dogs according to modern international standards.

The center features both outdoor and indoor training areas, designed to support work in all weather conditions and for different types of training — from obedience and sport disciplines to protection and K9 training.

In my home region, in the heart of Šumadija surrounded by untouched nature, a space was created that combines experience gained around the world, a natural environment, and functional infrastructure — with one goal: to provide every dog with the best possible conditions for development, work, and progress.

What is Simpor Dog Resort

More Than
Boarding — It's Their Vacation

Simpor Dog Resort is an exclusive part of Simpor Dog Center, created for owners who want their dogs to enjoy the highest level of care, comfort, and safety while they are away. Whether you are traveling, on vacation, or simply need daily care for your dog, Simpor Dog Resort offers a unique environment where every dog receives professional attention, individual care, and the feeling of being at home.

Located in the heart of nature, within more than two hectares of organized and secure space, Simpor Dog Resort combines premium accommodation, professional supervision, and daily activities designed to keep every dog happy, relaxed, and active.

Our goal is simple — while you enjoy your vacation, your dog will enjoy theirs.

What We Offer

Built for the
Best Dogs

01
Premium Boarding

Spacious, clean, and climate-controlled accommodations designed for the comfort and well-being of dogs of all sizes and temperaments. Individual suites for sport and working dogs.

02
Training Maintenance

Keep your dog's training sharp during their stay. Our certified handlers continue working on skills and commands, so your dog returns home better than they left.

03
Socialization

Supervised social interaction with other dogs, designed for each dog's personality and temperament. Essential enrichment for working dogs during their rest periods.

04
Recovery & Rehab

Specialized recovery programs for sport dogs following competitions, alongside rehabilitation support for dogs recovering from injury or surgery.

05
Veterinary Coordination

Close cooperation with trusted veterinary professionals. Health monitoring throughout every stay, with immediate action protocols and owner reporting.

06
Nutrition Plans

Custom feeding schedules using premium nutrition tailored to each dog's breed, age, working level, and health requirements. We follow your dog's existing diet exactly.
