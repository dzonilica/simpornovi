Who We Are

A Legacy Built
on Excellence

Simpor Dog Center is a professional center for the breeding, training, and development of working and sport dogs, built on more than 30 years of experience in cynology. Our work includes the selection, breeding, and training of dogs for sport, personal protection, official service purposes, and family life, with a clear focus on stable temperament, reliability, and outstanding results.

By combining international experience, proven training methods, and modern working standards, Simpor Dog Center has built a reputation as a recognized name on the international canine scene. Our mission is to provide every dog and owner with professional support and to create a strong, functional bond between human and dog.
