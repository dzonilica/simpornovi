Born to Be Great

The puppies from our breeding program are the result of careful parent selection and many years of experience working with sport and working bloodlines. Special attention is given to health, temperament, and the proper development of every puppy.

Buyers receive professional guidance when choosing a puppy, along with advice and support for further raising and training. We do not simply hand over a puppy — we guide you through the entire process, from choosing the right puppy for your goals, to support throughout its development.

All litters are produced from KKL-certified parents with full FCI documentation, health clearances, and proper breed evaluation. Every puppy is temperament tested and assessed for working potential before leaving our care.
