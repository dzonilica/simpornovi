Our People

One Family.
Many Countries.

Today, Simpor Dog Center represents much more than one person or one place. Behind every result, every dog, and every success stands a team of dedicated people, together with a large number of friends and partners from Serbia and around the world.

Through years of work, competition, training, and breeding, a strong network of professionals, trainers, judges, handlers, and dog enthusiasts has been built — people whose knowledge, experience, and support have contributed to the development of Simpor Dog Center. Many of them are part of our everyday work, while others have left their mark through joint projects, cooperation, and the exchange of knowledge.

Shared values and the same passion for dogs have connected people from different continents into one large family. It is precisely thanks to this team and these friendships that Simpor Dog Center is where it is today.
